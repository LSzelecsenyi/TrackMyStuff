STRICT

Delete account

Use this page if you no longer have the Strict app installed. It deletes the Strict account for the Google account you verify here. An email address on its own is not enough.

What deletion does

  • Deletes the Strict account on the server, including Founder records and promotional grants tied to it.
  • Does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Billing stays with Google until you cancel it there.
  • Does not restore a purchase onto a new Strict account.
  • Does not delete a ZIP backup you already exported. That file is on your device or wherever you saved it.
  • Does not normally undo. Signing in again creates a new empty account.

Manage Google Play subscriptions

This server is not configured to verify a Google account, so deletion cannot be started here.

A paid Google Play subscription is known for this account. Deleting Strict does not cancel it.

If you pay for Strict through Google Play, cancel that subscription in Google Play. This page cannot cancel it.
